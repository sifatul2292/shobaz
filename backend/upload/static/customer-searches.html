  • Counts searches typed in the shop's header search box (2 letters or more). Typing step by step (“rich”, “rich dad”) within a minute counts once. Opening a /products?q= link directly is not counted, because that page searches inside the browser.
  • “People” counts different visitors per day; no names, phone numbers or IP addresses are kept. Staff searches from the admin panel and bots are not counted.
  • “No results” means the shop search found nothing for those words. Spelling mistakes show up here too, so check the search in the shop () before stocking a book.
  • Times are Dhaka time. Searches are kept for 12 months.

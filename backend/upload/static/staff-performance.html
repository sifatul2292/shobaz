Points per action

—

  • Each order counts once per action, for the staff member who did it first. Re-sending, double clicks or switching a status back and forth never add points.
  • “Incomplete converted” is the Incomplete Orders page’s Send / Add Order; “Customer Manager order” is Create order in a customer’s popup. Both count when that order’s parcel is sent to the courier, so cancelled orders never score.
  • “Extra books” are the books in the parcel beyond what the customer chose: the abandoned cart for an incomplete order, or the order before the first staff edit for any other order. They go to the staff member who added them, when the parcel is sent. Free gifts do not count and a package counts as one; Customer Manager orders score as a whole, without extra books.
  • Status: Top performer most points; Needs a push under 40% of the leader once the leader has 10+; Behind the rest; No points yet. The owner’s account is shown but never ranked.
  • Changing the points recalculates every period. Actions are recorded from the day this feature went live.
